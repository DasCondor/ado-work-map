1. Capacity check-in

Everyone sets their hours per day and any time off in this sprint — a full day, half a day, or a few hours. Team-wide days off (holidays) apply to everyone. Capacity is what's left.
Assigned work (task hours)Estimated from points (no tasks yet)Capacity line
🔄

2. Carry over

What didn't finish last sprint? Pick what comes along — its remaining task hours count against capacity straight away.
🃏

3. Planning poker

On one shared screen: each person picks a card (or tells the facilitator), then reveal. Votes aren't synced between computers. Close votes become the estimate; far-apart votes mean talk it through and vote again. Items without an estimate come first.

4. Fill the sprint

Drag stories from the backlog onto a person (or use +). Their tasks come along; each lane shows assigned work against that person's capacity.

5. Confidence vote

Fist of five, entered on this screen: how confident is each of you that the team can finish this plan? 1 = no way, 5 = easy. An average of 3.5 or more and you're committed.
